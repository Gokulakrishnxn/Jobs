India Job Market Visualizer

A visualizer of ~440 occupations from India's NCO-2015, covering ~360M workers. Tile area is employment; color is the selected metric.

Why the layers look different from the US version

India has no single source like the US Bureau of Labor Statistics' Occupational Outlook Handbook — nothing publishes job duties, pay, education, and growth projections together per occupation. So this site assembles the closest real government sources for each piece, and is explicit about which layers are official data vs. LLM estimates:

  • Employment (tile size) — the detailed occupational breakdown comes from the Census of India 2011 (table B-25A plus Primary Census Abstract cultivator/agricultural-labourer totals). Each Division's total is rescaled to its real 2023-24 share from the PLFS Annual Report (Table 25). About 80 of ~440 NCO-2015 occupations could not be matched to older Census codes and sit in a per-Division "unclassified" tile.
  • Est. Annual Pay — anchored to real PLFS 2023-24 figures: regular wage/salaried earnings by NCO-2015 Division (Table 50); self-employed and casual labour use national PLFS averages. An LLM then adjusts within that anchor per occupation.
  • Growth Outlook and Digital AI Exposure — fully LLM-estimated (Gemini). India has no official per-occupation growth projections.

    FAQ

    What is this site?

    A research visualizer of India’s labour market. Each rectangle is an occupation: area is how many people work in it, color is the layer you pick (growth outlook, pay, education, or AI exposure). It is a development tool, not an official report or forecast.

    Where does the data come from?

    There is no single India source like the US BLS handbook, so layers are assembled from official files plus LLM estimates:

    • Names and descriptions — NCO-2015 (Ministry of Labour), ~440 occupation families.
    • Tile size (employment) — Census of India 2011 (table B-25A + Primary Census Abstract), with each Division rescaled to its real 2023-24 share from the PLFS Annual Report (Table 25).
    • Pay anchors — PLFS 2023-24 Table 50 (regular wage/salaried by Division). Self-employed and casual labour use national PLFS averages.
    • Growth outlook and AI exposure — LLM estimates (Gemini). India has no official per-occupation growth projections.
    Why is employment based on Census 2011?

    2011 is India’s most recent full census with a detailed occupation breakdown. The shape of occupations inside each Division comes from 2011; the scale of each Division is updated to PLFS 2023-24 so totals reflect today’s workforce mix.

    Why are some occupations missing or “unclassified”?

    Census used older NCO-2004 codes. About 80 of ~440 NCO-2015 occupations could not be matched and sit in a per-Division “unclassified” tile. Another ~25.5M workers whose Census response was not reported appear as Occupation Not Reported. Total employment still adds up to the Census figure.

    What do the color layers mean?
    • Growth Outlook — estimated % change in employment. Green is growing, red is declining. LLM estimate, not a government projection.
    • Est. Annual Pay — PLFS Division/national wage anchors, with an LLM adjustment per occupation.
    • Education — typical education tier for the occupation (LLM-assigned).
    • Digital AI Exposure — 0–10 score for how much current (mostly digital) AI may reshape the work. High does not mean the job disappears.
    Does a high AI exposure score mean the job will vanish?

    No. A high score means the work is more exposed to digital AI (writing, coding, analysis). Demand can still grow if people become more productive. The score ignores regulation, social preference for human workers, and new demand the model cannot see.

    How do I find a specific occupation?

    Use the search bar (for example “Cultivators”). Matching tiles stay bright on the map. Click or tap a result — or a tile — to open the full detail sheet: description, workers, pay, outlook, education, and notes.

    Can I treat this as an official forecast?

    No. Growth, AI exposure, and the per-occupation pay tweak are exploratory LLM estimates. Official pieces are the NCO taxonomy, Census employment counts, and PLFS wage/share anchors. Read the colored layers as a lens, not a prediction.
